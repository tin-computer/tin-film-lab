# LocIn CLI feature film

| Field | Choice |
|---|---|
| Feature | CLI: code strings become keys and language files. |
| Viewer / action | Developers and indie hackers → try the CLI. |
| Format | X, 20 seconds, 16:9; preview 1280 × 720 at 60 fps. |
| Look | Art plus a short product example; Loci and paper code shapes. |

## Choices and review

Hamit answered three questions: CLI code-to-language-file feature; developers / indie hackers trying the CLI; X, 20 seconds horizontal, art + short product example, Loci and rhythmic music. He approved the story and three-frame direction, then requested the music preview with more visual action and less text.

The current film follows those choices. Visual feedback on the final music preview remains open. Render checks passed; they do not replace that review.
